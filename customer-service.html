Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my order?

Go to Your Orders to view current progress, carrier tracking number, and estimated arrival date.

What is the return policy?

Most items fulfilled by Amazon can be returned within 30 days of receipt in original condition for a full refund.

How do I cancel an order?

Visit Your Orders before shipment and select Cancel items. If already shipped, you can refuse delivery or initiate a return.

How do refunds work?

Refunds are credited to your original payment method or Amazon Pay balance within 3 to 5 business days after inspection.
